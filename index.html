What are you adding?

Outlet (Back of House License) A physical location with its own address. Every one includes one cost center.
CK add on An add on to an Outlet (Back of House) license that turns it into a central kitchen.
WH add on An add on to an Outlet (Back of House) license that turns it into a warehouse.
Additional cost center For a location running more than one kitchen, CK or warehouse. Add one for each cost center beyond the first.
