Outlet and cost center details

Add a row for each one you are adding. Outlets and cost centers can be mixed in the same request.
Not sure which type to pick?
Restaurant / Outlet
Sells to guests and consumes stock. Has its own POS, stock counts and supplier orders.
Central Kitchen
Produces food and sends it to other locations. Runs recipes and batch production, and issues internal transfers rather than selling to guests.
Warehouse / Store
Receives and distributes stock without producing or selling it. Takes supplier deliveries, then issues to branches.
Cost center
Not a location of its own. A sub unit inside an existing branch, such as a bar, banquet or staff meals, used to split cost and consumption within that branch.
Not sure
Pick this and describe the setup in Details. We will advise on the right structure.
The quickest test: if it holds its own stock and gets counted separately, it is a branch, and which of the three depends on whether it sells, produces or stores. If it shares another branch's stock and you only need to see its costs apart, it is a cost center.

Add as many branches or cost centers as you need.
  • Name. The name your team will recognise in Supy.
  • Type. An outlet is a site of your own. A cost center sits inside one:
    Outlet
    a restaurant or store that serves guests and consumes stock.
    Central Kitchen
    an outlet that produces and transfers stock to your other sites, running recipes and batch production.
    Warehouse
    an outlet that holds and issues stock to other sites, with no production of its own.
    Cost center
    a stock holding unit inside an existing outlet, such as a bar, pastry section or banquet kitchen. It counts and costs separately but shares the outlet address.
  • Belongs to outlet. Cost centers only. Pick one of the outlets you are adding above, or type an existing outlet name as it appears in your account. Outlets leave this blank.
  • Address. Street, area, city and country, up to 200 characters. This appears on purchase orders and delivery notes, so outlets need it and cost centers do not.
  • Clone setup from. Copying an existing one is faster than building from scratch, so name the closest match on menu and suppliers. Type it as it appears in your account. Write "none" to set up fresh.
  • Details. Which locations it supplies or is supplied by, target go live date, the day close time if it differs from your other sites, and anything that should differ from the setup you are copying.
